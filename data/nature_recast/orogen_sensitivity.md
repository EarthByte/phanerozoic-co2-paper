# Erosion sensitivity of mountain-building events on the main model

Tracked crust as in orogen_tracks_before_after.md. a = ln(flux ratio)/ln(erosion-flux ratio) from the age before uplift to the age of the belt: "realised" with the simulated climate of both ages, "fixed climate" with the climate of one age applied to the erosion rates of both (mean of both orders). Net = silicate sink minus sulfide source; net with OC adds biospheric burial and subtracts rock organic-carbon oxidation. Changes in net drawdown in t C km⁻² yr⁻¹ of tracked crust.
