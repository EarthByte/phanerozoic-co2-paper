# Mountain belts followed through time on the main model

Crust above 1,500 m at the age of each belt, tracked through the PALEOMAP plate model; fluxes per km² of that crust (t C km⁻² yr⁻¹), net = silicate sink minus sulfide source. "Before" is the age, within the 60 Myr before the belt, at which the land of the tracked crust stood lowest (its mean elevation); "after" is 60 Myr after the belt age.
