# Mountain belts followed through time on the main model

Crust above 1,500 m at the age of each belt, tracked through the PALEOMAP plate model; fluxes per km² of that crust (t C km⁻² yr⁻¹), net = silicate sink minus sulfide source. "Before" is the age, within the 60 Myr before the belt, at which the land of the tracked crust stood lowest (its mean elevation); "after" is 60 Myr after the belt age.

## Caledonian belt, 420 Ma

Before (465 Ma) → belt (420 Ma). Land share of the tracked crust 0.17 → 1.00; mean elevation 159 → 2210 m; mean erosion rate 0.051 → 0.440 mm/yr; runoff 911 → 1468 mm/yr; median E/E_opt 0.44 → 3.99 (quartiles 0.28–0.70 → 3.13–5.00); share beyond the optimum 0.15 → 0.99; net drawdown 0.195 → 1.514 t C km⁻² yr⁻¹ (silicate 0.217 → 2.438); S_net +0.22 → -0.58.
Change in net drawdown per km² of tracked crust +1.319 t C km⁻² yr⁻¹: -0.016 from the change in erosion and -0.074 from the change in runoff and temperature on the crust that is land at both ages (17% of it), +1.409 from crust that emerged from (or sank below) sea level.
After (360 Ma): median E/E_opt 4.04, share beyond 0.98, net drawdown 0.432 t C km⁻² yr⁻¹, S_net -0.49.
